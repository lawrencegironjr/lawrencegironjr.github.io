2024 Alaska Space Grant Education and Research Symposium

NASA Langley Research Center Academy & NASA Glenn Research Center Internship

The Alaska Space Grant Program and Alaska NASA EPSCoR Education and Research Symposium is a multidisciplinary exhibition of the research and projects conducted by Alaskan undergraduate students, graduate students and faculty. The purpose of the Symposium is to share the amazing NASA-relevant research and projects occurring across Alaska and allow students and faculty to foster inter-university collaboration and ideas.
